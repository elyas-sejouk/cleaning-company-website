# Éclat du Sud Context

This context represents the static website and quotation platform for "Éclat du Sud", a professional cleaning service operating in the French Arc Méditerranéen.

## Language

**Éclat du Sud**:
The official brand and business name representing the cleaning service.
_Avoid_: Éclat de Sud, Eclat du Sud (without accent), Nettoyage Sud

**Client**:
The business (e.g., Airbnb host, property manager, construction site supervisor) or individual requesting cleaning services.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, account

**Prestation**:
A specific category of cleaning service offered by the company (e.g., Airbnb cleaning, post-construction cleaning, residential cleaning).
_Avoid_: Service, job, mission, task

**Devis**:
A custom quotation or proposal generated for a client based on their requested prestations and project details.
_Avoid_: Quote, estimation, billing request

**Zone d'Intervention**:
The designated geographic municipalities (e.g., Narbonne, Béziers, Gruissan) where Éclat du Sud delivers cleaning prestations for clients.
_Avoid_: Coverage area, service territory, city list

**Réalisation**:
One completed job, already delivered for one client at one location, comprising one or more prestations and evidenced by photographic proof. A prestation is what Éclat du Sud sells; a réalisation is a specific occasion on which it was delivered.
_Avoid_: Mission, chantier, intervention, project, case study, portfolio item

**Avant/Après**:
A matched pair of photographs of the same subject within a réalisation, one taken before the work and one after, presented together as evidence of the transformation.
_Avoid_: Before/after, comparison, slider, transformation

**Mi-Parcours**:
A single photograph taken with the work deliberately half-finished, in which cleaned and uncleaned surface meet inside the same frame. Distinct from [[avant-apres]]: the evidence is the boundary itself rather than the relationship between two images, so it needs no pairing, no caption, and cannot be assembled after the fact.
_Avoid_: Half-cleaned shot, split shot, work-in-progress photo, demo shot
